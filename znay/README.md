# Znay — Find Your Next Business Opportunity

**Znay** is a B2B platform designed to help businesses find companies, discover potential clients, and identify new business opportunities in Ukraine.

The goal is to make business discovery easier by bringing company information into one convenient place.

## 🎯 Goal

Build a platform that helps entrepreneurs, sales teams, and businesses find relevant companies without spending hours searching across multiple websites and databases.

## ✨ Planned Features

- 🔎 **Company Search** — find businesses by name and available information.
- 🏢 **Company Directory** — explore companies operating in Ukraine.
- 📍 **Location Filtering** — narrow search results by city or region.
- 🏷️ **Category Filtering** — find companies based on their business activities where data is available.
- 📇 **Contact Information** — access available business contact details and company websites.
- ⭐ **Lead Management** — save interesting companies to personal lists.
- 📤 **Data Export** — export selected company data for further work.
- 🔄 **Data Updates** — track data sources and when information was last updated.

*Features are planned and may change during development.*

## 👥 Who Is It For?

Znay is being developed for:

- Entrepreneurs looking for potential clients and partners.
- Sales teams searching for new leads.
- Marketing agencies researching businesses.
- Freelancers looking for potential customers.
- Companies exploring new markets.

## 🌍 Initial Market

The first target market is **Ukraine**, with the possibility of expanding to other countries in the future.

## 🔎 Company Data Sources

Znay will collect and combine business information from multiple sources to help users discover relevant companies in Ukraine.

### Planned Data Sources

- **[Ukrainian Unified State Register (EDR)](https://data.gov.ua/dataset/03cc1239-3988-4451-aa0d-aadb77448714)** — official information about registered legal entities and individual entrepreneurs, subject to dataset availability and access restrictions.
- **[OpenStreetMap](https://www.openstreetmap.org/)** — geographic and business information, including locations and points of interest, subject to its open-data license.
- **Official Company Websites** — publicly available business information, such as company descriptions, services, websites, and published contact details, where collection and use are permitted.
- **[Opendatabot API](https://opendatabot.ua/open/api)** — a potential source for company data enrichment and additional information about registered businesses. Commercial access and data-use terms must be reviewed before integration.
- **[Prozorro](https://prozorro.gov.ua/)** — public procurement data that may help identify companies participating in tenders and discover potential B2B opportunities.

### How Company Search Will Work

Znay aims to combine information from these sources into a searchable company directory.

Users will be able to search and filter available records by:
- Company name
- City or region
- Business category or activity, when available
- Available company contact details
- Other relevant business attributes as the dataset grows

Each record should retain its source information and, where possible, the date it was collected or last updated.

### Data Quality

Znay will aim to:
- Normalize company names and other available fields.
- Detect and merge duplicate company records.
- Track the original source of each record.
- Identify outdated or incomplete information.
- Validate contact details where technically and legally appropriate.

### Data & Privacy

Znay will use data sources according to their applicable licenses, terms of service, and legal requirements.

The platform will not assume that publicly accessible information can automatically be copied, stored, exported, or resold. Personal data will be handled in accordance with applicable privacy and data-protection requirements.

The availability and completeness of company information will depend on each source and its access restrictions.
